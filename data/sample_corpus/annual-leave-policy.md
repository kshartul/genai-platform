# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month.

## Carry forward
Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or they lapse.

## Requesting leave
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than 3 days. Managers approve or reject requests within 3 working days.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year; a medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.
