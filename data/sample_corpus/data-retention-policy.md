# Data Retention Policy

## Retention periods
Customer transaction records are retained for 8 years to meet audit requirements. Application logs are retained for 90 days in hot storage and 1 year in archive storage. Marketing consent records are kept for 3 years after the last interaction.

## Deletion
Personal data must be deleted within 30 days of a verified erasure request. Backups containing deleted data expire on their normal 35-day rotation.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data must be encrypted at rest with customer-managed keys.
