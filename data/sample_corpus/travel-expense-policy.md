# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking travel
All flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.

## Daily allowances
The daily meal allowance is INR 2,500 for domestic travel and USD 75 for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Submitting expenses
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the trip end date, with itemised receipts for any single expense above INR 1,000. Claims submitted after 60 days are rejected.

## Approvals
Expenses up to INR 50,000 are approved by the line manager. Expenses above INR 50,000 also need Finance Controller approval.
